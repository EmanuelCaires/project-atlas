# Atlas Product Principles

## Our Mission

Atlas exists to help developers demonstrate their abilities through real evidence and guide them towards stronger professional opportunities.

---

## 1. Evidence Over Claims

We value demonstrated work more than self-declared skills.

Projects, deployments, documentation and contributions build trust.

---

## 2. Build for Tomorrow. Deliver Today.

Every feature should solve today's problem while supporting tomorrow's vision.

Avoid shortcuts that create technical debt.

---

## 3. One Clear Next Step

Developers should never wonder what to do next.

Atlas always provides one meaningful recommendation.

---

## 4. Explain Every Score

Every score must be transparent.

Users should understand:

- why they received it
- how it was calculated
- how to improve it

---

## 5. Encourage, Never Discourage

Atlas is a coach.

Not a judge.

Recommendations should motivate improvement rather than punish missing information.

---

## 6. Progress Should Be Visible

Developers should see themselves improving over time.

Small achievements deserve recognition.

---

## 7. Trust Is Earned

Every feature should increase trust between developers and employers.

If it doesn't improve trust, it should be questioned.

---

## 8. Quality Over Quantity

Ten weak projects are not better than three excellent ones.

Atlas rewards evidence, not volume.

---

## 9. Simplicity Wins

If a feature needs a long explanation, it probably needs redesigning.

Users should immediately understand how Atlas helps them.

---

## 10. Help People Grow

Atlas should improve careers.

Not simply store profiles.

Every recommendation should move the developer forward.

---

# Atlas Philosophy

We believe careers should be built on evidence.

We believe opportunities should be created through demonstrated ability.

We believe everyone deserves the chance to show what they can build.

Build for tomorrow.

Deliver today.

---

## 11. Create Fair Opportunities

Atlas helps reduce reliance on weak hiring signals by increasing the visibility of meaningful professional evidence.

The goal is not to replace interviews.

The goal is to ensure that demonstrated ability is seen before assumptions are made.

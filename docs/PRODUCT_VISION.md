# Atlas Product Vision

Version: 1.0

---

# Mission

Atlas helps technology professionals prove their capabilities through evidence instead of claims.

Rather than asking developers to list technologies they know, Atlas enables them to demonstrate real-world experience using projects, code, deployments, certifications and measurable achievements.

---

# Vision

Become the world's most trusted professional identity platform for technology professionals.

Atlas will become the place where employers, recruiters and clients verify capability through evidence rather than relying on CVs, self-assessments or keyword matching.

---

# The Problem

Today's hiring process has several major problems.

Developers struggle to prove what they can actually do.

Recruiters receive hundreds of CVs that all look similar.

GitHub measures code.

LinkedIn measures careers.

Portfolios measure presentation.

None of these platforms combine evidence into a complete professional identity.

Atlas exists to solve this.

---

# Our Philosophy

Evidence over claims.

Quality over quantity.

Trust over popularity.

Real work over buzzwords.

Professional growth over static profiles.

# Core Principles

## Every skill requires evidence.

A skill without evidence has limited value.

Atlas should always encourage users to connect skills to real projects.

---

## Every project tells a story.

Projects should explain:

- the problem
- the solution
- technologies used
- personal contribution
- outcomes

---

## Professional identity evolves.

Atlas is never "finished."

It grows as a person's career grows.

---

## Trust is earned.

Evidence should become increasingly verifiable over time.

Examples:

- GitHub
- Live deployments
- Certifications
- Employer verification
- Open-source contributions
- Technical assessments

# Atlas Score

Future versions of Atlas will calculate an Evidence Score.

The score will never be based on popularity.

Instead it will consider:

- evidence quality
- evidence diversity
- evidence recency
- verified sources
- consistency

Atlas becomes the trusted professional identity layer for the global technology workforce.

---

# Success Metrics

A successful Atlas user should be able to:

- prove their technical ability
- find better opportunities
- reduce reliance on traditional CVs
- build long-term professional credibility

A successful employer should be able to:

- hire faster
- reduce hiring risk
- discover stronger candidates
- evaluate capability through evidence

# Founder Principle

Every feature added to Atlas must answer one question:
